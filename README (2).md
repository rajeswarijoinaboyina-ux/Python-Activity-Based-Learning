1.  **University Scholarship Allocation**

    -   Rank eligible students by higher average, higher attendance,
        lower income, then name.
2.  **Progressive Electricity Billing**
    -   Calculate the electricity bill using units consumed and the
        given slabs.
    -   Add the fixed charge of ₹50 and 5% surcharge.
    -   Apply a ₹20 rebate when usage is below 100 units; check the
        increase against the previous month's bill.
3.  **Hospital Billing and Patient Classification**
    -   Calculate the bill from consultation, diagnostic, and treatment
        charges.
    -   Reject negative charges or payment.
    -   Give a 10% discount when the total is above ₹5,000; classify the
        payment as Fully Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid.
4.  **Employee Payroll and Deduction Analysis**
    -   Calculate gross salary from basic pay, allowance, overtime, and
        incentive.
    -   Deduct tax progressively: 5% on the first ₹20,000, 10% on the
        next ₹20,000, and 15% above ₹40,000.
    -   Calculate net salary.
5.  **Multi-Plan Telecommunications Cost Optimization**
    -   Compare plans using usage, required validity, budget, data
        allowance, and extra data charges.
    -   Calculate each plan's total cost and select the lowest-cost plan

6.  **Railway Fare and Reservation Calculator**
    -   Calculate fare using passenger age, distance, and class.
    -   Sleeper rate: ₹2/km; AC rate: ₹5/km.

        stated cancellation rule.
7.  **Retail Billing with Multiple Discount Rules**

8.  **Manufacturing Cost and Profitability Analysis**
    -   Calculate wastage cost from material cost and wastage
        percentage.

        costs.
    -   Calculate profit and compare it with the target.
9.  **Multi-Currency Travel Budget Analysis**
    -   Convert expenses using the exchange rate and conversion fee
        percentage.
    -   Calculate total converted expenses, identify the highest expense
        category, and compare the total with the INR budget.
10. **Campus Event Financial Planning**
    -   Calculate total event cost from venue, equipment, catering,
        transport, and certificates.
